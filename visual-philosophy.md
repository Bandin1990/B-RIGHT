# Chromatic Due Process

The visual language is built as a calm, legible system: a single upstream spine carries every case into a deliberate assessment, then opens into three distinct chromatic lanes. Space is used as a promise of fairness—each lane receives equal visual dignity, while the shared beginning and shared outcome make the process easy to follow.

Color is functional rather than ornamental. Green signals low risk and immediate release; yellow signals a measured need for security; red signals the highest level of scrutiny and activated assistance. Muted fields, dark ink, and thin graphite connectors keep the diagram authoritative and suitable for official use.

Rounded forms soften the institutional language without weakening it. The rhythm moves from intake, to information, to assessment, to outcome. Decision points are expressed by branching geometry, not by dense explanation. The design is meticulously crafted so that a reader can trace any path with one glance.

Typography is sparse and hierarchical: a clear title, short action labels, and concise lane notes. Every alignment, gap, arrow, and color transition is treated with painstaking attention. The final artifact should feel like the product of deep expertise—quiet, precise, and labored over until the logic is unmistakable.
