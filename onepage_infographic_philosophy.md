# Chromatic Justice Map

The visual system treats due process as a map: one clear spine establishes the universal steps, then color opens three measured pathways. The composition is calm and civic, using generous space, rounded modules, and precise connectors so the reader can understand the mechanism before reading every label.

Green, amber, and red are not decoration; they are the information architecture. Green carries the promise of rapid release, amber carries the work of matching risk with a guarantee, and red carries the legal safeguard of a reasoned decision and court process when detention must continue. Blue identifies the shared data and support infrastructure.

Typography is concise and hierarchical. A strong title establishes the innovation, short cards name the problem and the three engines, and the flow diagram does the explanatory work. Every connector is deliberately aligned, every color is restrained, and every line of text earns its place.

The final page is meticulously crafted as an executive briefing artifact: quick to scan from a distance, rigorous enough to withstand close reading, and polished with the painstaking attention of a master information designer.
